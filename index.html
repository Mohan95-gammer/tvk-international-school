About Our School

Sunrise Public School is committed to academic excellence, character development, and holistic growth. Established in 2002, we combine innovative teaching, modern infrastructure, and a warm community so every student can discover their talents and grow into confident, responsible citizens. Our mission is to nurture curiosity, discipline, and creativity in every learner.
